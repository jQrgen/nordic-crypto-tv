# Nordic Crypto app: privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

The Nordic Crypto app for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Vision Pro and Apple TV is made by Jørgen S. Notland (jQrgen).

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not collect, store or share personal data.

## What the app connects to

- **Nordic Crypto data** (`cryptonordic.no`, `jqrgen.github.io` and `raw.githubusercontent.com`): the app downloads the public news, events and newsletter files. These are ordinary web requests; we do not run code on these servers that identifies you.
- **Newsletter video** (`github.com`): the newsreel is streamed from a public GitHub release when you choose to play it.
- **Radio Norge** (Apple TV only, `live-bauerno.sharp-stream.com`): the radio stream is played from the broadcaster's public stream while the radio is on. You can switch it off with Play/Pause on the remote.

Like any web request, these services see your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply to that.

## What stays on your device

The app keeps a copy of the latest news so it works offline, and on Apple TV it remembers whether you switched the radio off. Both stay on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

## Links and QR codes

Stories link to the original publishers, and the newsletter links to Substack. When you open those links or scan the QR codes, you leave the app and the destination's privacy policy applies.
